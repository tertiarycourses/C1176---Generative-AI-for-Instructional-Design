# Tools & Setup — Generative AI for Instructional Design (C1176)

Everything you need for the 10 hands-on labs. Every tool used in this course is
**web-based and cloud-powered** — there is nothing to install beyond a modern
browser. You use two kinds of tool together: **general chat assistants** for the
analysis, writing and design thinking, and an **AI image tool** for the visuals
and job aids.

## Accounts & software

| Tool | What it's for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Web browser** (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) | Runs every tool in this course | Keep it up to date |
| **A chat assistant** — ChatGPT (chat.openai.com), Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) | Needs analysis, objectives, curriculum and lesson plans, content, activities, quizzes, rubrics, scripts, alignment and review | A free account for any one of them is enough; the trainer will confirm what's available |
| **An AI image tool** — image generation in ChatGPT / Gemini / Microsoft Copilot, or Adobe Firefly / Canva | Generating on-brand visuals and job aids (Lab 8) | Any one of these is enough |
| **Internet connection** | Every generative feature runs in the cloud | Required for every step |
| **A documents / notes app** | Keeping every reviewed output as your course kit | Any word processor or notes tool works |

## What each tool does in this course

| Tool | What you do with it | Labs |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini** | Draft prompts, analyse the need and audience, write objectives, structure the curriculum and lesson plans, generate content, activities and scenarios, create quizzes, tasks and rubrics, write scripts, run the alignment and quality check, and assemble the package | 1–10 |
| **An AI image tool** (ChatGPT / Gemini / Copilot / Firefly / Canva) | Generate on-brand visuals for the e-learning screens and job aids | 1, 8 |

## The AI workflow you'll use

Every lab follows the same loop — **prompt → review → refine** — and the course
as a whole follows one instructional-design workflow:

**Analyse → Design → Develop → Align → Deliver.**

You supply and verify the real context, facts and pedagogical judgement; the AI
drafts and suggests; you own every objective, every claim and the instructional
quality of the finished course.

## Before Lab 1 — confirm your setup

1. Open and **sign in** to at least one chat assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini).
2. Open and **sign in** to an **AI image tool** (image generation in ChatGPT / Gemini / Copilot, or Firefly / Canva).
3. Send a simple prompt (for example *"Hello, are you ready to help me design a training course?"*) and confirm you get a reply.
4. Create a project folder called **`Meridian-Service-Course-Kit`** for all your work.
5. Open the **Meridian training brief** in [reference-pack/](reference-pack/).

If any of these is missing, tell the trainer at the start of the day.

## Responsible & quality use

- Do **not** paste confidential employee data, personal information or proprietary training material into a public AI tool — use the supplied Meridian training brief.
- Treat every AI output — **especially facts, examples and assessment answers** — as a first draft to review, fact-check with a subject expert, and check for bias.
- Confirm every artifact **aligns to a learning objective** before it goes into the package, and be transparent about AI assistance (including AI-generated images) where appropriate before the course reaches real learners.

## The connected project

Every lab builds one part of the **Meridian Service Excellence course kit** (see
[reference-pack/](reference-pack/)). Keep everything in your
`Meridian-Service-Course-Kit` folder so the course package stays together and
